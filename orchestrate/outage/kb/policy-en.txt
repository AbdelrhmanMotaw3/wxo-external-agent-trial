KAI Synthetic Internet Outage Data Compensation Policy
Policy ID: OUTAGE-DATA-2026.11-v1. Effective demo snapshot: 18 November 2026.
This is a fictional event policy, not a real operator policy or a statement of law.

INDEX: S1 eligibility; S2 bonus data; S3 human approval; S4 provisioning and invoice; S5 evidence and privacy; S6 communication.

S1 — Eligibility and exclusions
A restored, unplanned provider network outage affecting an active home-internet service is eligible after at least 24 whole confirmed hours. Require service and incident IDs, confirmed start and restoration timestamps, cause, scope and source reference. Estimated restoration is not confirmation. Customer router faults, billing suspensions and planned maintenance are excluded. Missing evidence requires investigation.

S2 — Compensation amount
The event scenario grants one fixed 20 GB bonus data bundle per eligible case. A confirmed 72-hour outage therefore qualifies for a proposed 20 GB bundle. This is additional data on the affected home-internet service. No cash refund, invoice credit or cheaper subscription is included. No expiry date is specified for this demonstration; do not invent a validity period. The backend proposal is authoritative for the current award and bonus balance.

S3 — Human approval and execution
Every proposed 20 GB bundle requires a human decision in the native approval form. A chat message saying approved, a model claim or a customer request is not approval. Reject leaves bonus data unchanged. Approve triggers the workflow-only provisioning tool, which revalidates the stored proposal, evidence, policy and case versions. Expired proposals require reassessment. Repeated execution of the same accepted proposal returns the same receipt and never awards data twice. Another proposal cannot compensate the same case twice.

S4 — Bonus data and unchanged billing
Starting compensation data balance: 0 GB. Approved award: 20 GB. New compensation data balance: 20 GB. The outstanding invoice remains 900 EGP and the recurring plan remains 900 EGP per month. The compensation balance describes this bonus bundle, not the total plan allowance or remaining monthly quota. Rejection keeps compensation data at 0 GB and the invoice at 900 EGP. Provisioning is synthetic; no real operator network is changed.

S5 — Evidence and privacy
Retain the case, service, incident/source, policy reference, native decision and provisioning receipt. Retrieve and cite this policy before a policy conclusion. Missing retrieval must be disclosed rather than replaced with invented citations. Platform privacy controls remain separate from approval, authorization and optional Agent Identity. Minimize personal data and use the masked saved WhatsApp contact.

S6 — Customer communication
Confirm completion only after an actual posted compensation receipt. State service restoration, 20 GB bonus data added, the case and receipt, and the unchanged 900 EGP invoice. A proposal is not an award. Drafting is separate from sending. The event WhatsApp scenario transport returns an internal reference and makes no provider dispatch or real delivery claim.
